Pepforge Public Research Release v2.0.0 - Compound DB MW/Density Normalization Report

Scope
- Audited apps/spps_planner_app/data/compounds.csv.
- Separated reagent MW for material usage from product MW contribution for final peptide mass.
- Corrected Ac default: Acetic anhydride reagent MW 102.09 g/mol, acetyl product contribution 42.04 Da.
- Kept Caffeic acid / Caf at reagent MW 180.16 g/mol and caffeoyl contribution 162.14 Da.
- Corrected DIC density in settings_db.csv from 1.073 to 0.815 g/mL.
- Removed trailing unnamed column from compounds.csv.
- Added/normalized branch handles, non-natural AA, explicit label forms, peptide tags, and AA-like linkers.
- Generic/vendor-dependent labels, crosslinkers, and undefined click motifs are intentionally marked MANUAL_REQUIRED instead of assigning fake exact MW.

Current counts after normalization
- Active rows: 212
- Reagent MW blank/manual-required rows: 85
- Product MW contribution blank/manual-required rows: 37
- Manual-required audit: apps/spps_planner_app/data/compound_db_manual_required_audit.csv

Policy
- reagent_mw: actual weighed reagent / vendor form for material usage.
- product_contribution_mw: residue/cap/linker mass added to final peptide.
- liquid density is used only for liquid reagents/solvents.
- solid reagents keep density blank.
- generic dye/click/crosslinker names are not treated as exact chemical forms.
